# Companion race frame artwork

These original RGBA PNGs were created with the built-in ImageGen tool for the
SC2 Tools Companion. They are local application assets, not downloaded game UI
textures. Each source is 1254 × 1254 with an alpha-transparent center and exterior.

- `protoss-frame.png`: sculpted champagne-gold armor, cyan channels and crystals.
- `terran-frame.png`: blue-steel machinery, black hydraulic hoses, red lamps,
  green indicators and yellow/black corner markings.
- `zerg-frame.png`: fleshy plum/burgundy membranes, veins and sinew, protected by
  dark chitin and bone/ochre corner spines.

The runtime draws separate perimeter pieces, not a stretched whole image. Fixed
corners and center ornaments retain their proportions while plain edge spans
resize. The transparent center is never painted. Compact is a wide rectangular
340 × 78 DIP frame; Standard, Detailed and Full use the same materials with
independently fitted perimeter geometry. Default uses no generated artwork.

## Prompt set

### Protoss generation

Production-ready transparent PNG game UI frame artwork, intended as a nine-slice
resizable desktop companion border. One original polished Protoss-inspired
alien-gold frame, not a UI mockup. Front-facing orthographic square composition.
Reference direction: elegant layered gold architectural StarCraft BlizzCon
stage; sculpted champagne-gold metal, dark bronze recessed seams, engraved
flowing geometric alien motifs, luminous thin cyan energy channels and small
brilliant aquamarine faceted corner crystals. Rich physically rendered bevels,
believable specular highlights, dark carved undercuts and restrained AAA RTS
interface craftsmanship. Straight modular middle edges; ornament concentrated
near corners. Very large rectangular alpha-transparent center and transparent
exterior. Frame only: no text, numbers, logos, buttons, labels, display panel,
characters, scenery, stars or painted checkerboard. No glow across the center.

### Terran generation and approved blue-steel edit

Professional transparent PNG game interface border art for responsive perimeter
rendering. One original Terran-inspired industrial rectangular equipment frame,
front-facing orthographic. Huge alpha-transparent center and exterior. Layered
gunmetal and brushed titanium machined rails, bevelled steel corner clamps,
recessed black grooves, countersunk fasteners, subtly worn metal edges, slim
corrugated hydraulic hoses, restrained red/orange LEDs, small military yellow
and black corner markings and muted green diagnostic slits. Credible rendered
metal depth, crisp controlled highlights and modular straight middle rails.
No text, labels, logos, display, buttons, central objects, characters or scenery.

Approved edit: preserve exact composition, shape, hardware, hoses, fasteners,
warning stripes, red lamps, green diagnostics and full alpha transparency.
Recolor the armor to noticeably cooler blue-tinted steel: desaturated military
navy/slate-blue gunmetal, richer blue-grey anodized panels and cool icy blue
metallic edge reflections. Blue steel, not neutral grey, neon or plastic. Black
hoses remain black; machined highlights remain cool silver-blue. Preserve all
physically rendered detail and contrast; add no content or background.

### Zerg generation and approved fleshy edit

Production-quality transparent PNG game interface border art. One original
Zerg-inspired organic alien carapace frame, orthographic and rectangular with
a huge alpha-transparent center and transparent exterior. Dark brown and nearly
black chitin, bronze/ochre ridges, curved overlapping horn plates, sharp organic
corner spines, raised exoskeleton and controlled warm glowing seams. Four
sculpted corners connected by modular segmented carapace rails. Premium AAA RTS
interface materials and convincing biological detail. No text, symbols, buttons,
display content, background, eyes, skulls, heads or characters.

Approved edit: make it much more fleshy and alive, less like dry brown armor.
Preserve the canvas, silhouette, corner shapes, large rectangular opening and
genuine transparent center/exterior. Replace much of the hard rail surface with
glossy fleshy plum, muted mauve and dark burgundy membranes, ribbed sinewy cords,
branching fine purple veins, subtly translucent wet surfaces and small fleshy
nodules. Keep some dark chitin ribs and ochre/bone corner spines for contrast;
flesh dominates the connecting rails. Amber-orange bioluminescence only deep in
a few seams, not molten lava cracks everywhere. A grown Zerg brood structure,
not a stone or metal picture frame. No wounds, dripping blood, entrails, eyes,
faces, heads, bright green slime, text or interface content. Do not protrude new
tissue farther into the transparent opening.
