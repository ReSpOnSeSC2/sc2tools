## Controls and information boundary

Exactly eight starting Probes are retained. Camera movement, spatial selection and gameplay commands share the hard 200-input rolling-minute budget. The bot receives the current permitted screen, ordinary own HUD information and timestamped memory of earlier permitted observations. Current off-screen units, hidden enemies and opponent orders are excluded.

Static map expansion and geyser coordinates are prior map knowledge. Actual construction still requires camera coverage, current vision, available abilities and valid placement. Replay coordinates are not copied into live targets.

Native Windows screen capture/input is available through the computer-use runtime and has worked during active coaching. Use only the player's ordinary visible view, and distinguish any administrative/manual inputs from the bot's recorded audit. Do not inspect opponent telemetry, unfiltered engine state or a live replay while deciding strategy.

## Execution currently implemented

- Confirmed worker transfers retain their intended destination. A Probe observed arriving with a persistent Move-to-Nexus order receives a normal screen-selected Gather command to visible, nonempty minerals. Unknown orders and designated scout/build roles remain protected; in-flight workers count against destination capacity.
- Gas staffing is revisited when its last observation is at least 30 game seconds old, even if memory says 3/3. Camera visits remain paced and return to production; 30 seconds is eligibility, not a promised revisit deadline.
- Pending construction receives camera checks. A reservation is resolved only by permitted evidence, not an unseen worker's presumed death.
- One designated Probe can scout per game. Later idle Observers receive separate dispatches; Warp Prisms now remain with the army. A new spell controller queries real Recharge/Hallucination availability, confirms the cast, observes the newly produced hallucinated Phoenix, then scouts with it. Illusions never contribute real composition or army readiness. Optional Chrono is briefly deferred for the first observed/queued Sentry using a bounded public-duration allowance.
- Scout-camera checks are bounded: three empty arrival inspections with increasing delays, then no further visits until another confirmed dispatch or actual on-screen scout progress. No off-screen death is inferred.
- Army attention is interleaved with base upkeep. The local combat baseline focuses reachable visible threats, rotates unit types and attempts short retreats or cooldown kiting for endangered ranged units. It protects withdrawing units from an AllType reselection. A continuous combat view is capped at eight seconds before one observed-base visit and a three-second upkeep interval.
- Idle guard jobs run after productive macro: melee, ranged and support posts are now 10, 8 and at least 6.5 tiles from the exposed observed Nexus. The larger clearance avoids accidental follow-Nexus orders; only our own recorded army jobs with a matching observed follow order are recovered. Local positions require current screen, vision, pathing and observed-structure clearance. Remote rendezvous use remembered own bases and public map direction only.
- Pressure/attack assembles a shared cohort: at least 75% of the ordinary army-supply HUD, minimum six supply, must be observed nearby at departure. After v10, arrival uses 75% of the frozen departing wave within a six-tile current-screen footprint; later production cannot increase its quota while it moves. Camera attention follows the first confirmed command projection immediately and excludes old worker/scout recruitment positions. The natural remains the home rally when a third is built; explicit threatened-base defense still targets that base. Requested waypoints are at most eight tiles apart; emitted pixel projections are recorded separately. No arrival is inferred from elapsed time or acknowledgments alone.
- Ground attacks use ordinary Attack23 on currently visible, empty, pathable screen pixels. Offscreen intentions become short forward screen waypoints; ambiguous explicit enemy clicks fail closed. Native point Attack24 was rejected by the Zealot and is not used. Current-screen attacks targeting observed friendlies receive a normal gated Stop. Combat rendezvous now use this Attack23 path too, avoiding the observed minimap Move follow-unit loops. Worker/scout Move behavior is unchanged. Assembly freezes its initial HUD quota, relocates a newly obstructed rally only from current visible terrain, and receives a short action window during army-camera visits. The new assembly changes pass focused tests but still require a live advancing army.
- Warp Prism rescue runs before other micro. A current-screen Prism may pick up an endangered current-screen unit, then seek a locally safer visible unload. Selection is distinguished from observed cargo. Loaded Prisms and rescue targets are protected from generic army orders; empty Prisms follow the shared army anchor instead of scouting.
- Tactical priority is Prism rescue, Guardian Shield opportunity, conservative Force Field opportunity, then ordinary attack/kiting. Shield selection is not counted as a cast until engine confirmation. Force Fields require a currently visible narrow passage and conservative friendly-clearance/retreat checks; open terrain or unavailable public cast range fails closed. Reports expose confirmed spell commands and public cast-range diagnostics. v10 exercised this controller but did not establish reliable tactical strength.
- Expansion revisits now use a per-task ten-second cadence and a short global camera dwell. Frequent gas/base visits cannot indefinitely reset the old seven-second quiet requirement. The expansion report records its waiting reason, footprint visibility, available ability and actual placement result.
- Micro is a **basic scripted controller**, not a trained micro policy, battle predictor or demonstrated strong play. Production, placement, scouting, counter-compositions and combat still require live verification.

The latest micro considers several short retreat directions against current visible threats, preserves ready attacks, and avoids pulling distant or protected same-type units into a focus-fire selection. [MICRO_SOURCES.md](MICRO_SOURCES.md) records the Ares and Sharpy references and MIT licenses. The local implementation adapts ideas independently; no upstream framework or source file was copied. It is not the full Ares/Sharpy controller or a prediction of safety outside the screen.
