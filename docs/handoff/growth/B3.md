# B3 · Whine Permit

- **Built.** `/permit` compares typed wins and losses with the same-tier ladder cohort. Each permit shows the sample sizes, intervals and snapshot date. The shareable PNG includes only the selected matchup's typed aggregate and the ladder comparison. The site does not transmit or store the typed record.
- **Method.** Split the player’s deficit below 50% into the observed ladder gap and the remaining gap. Cap the ladder share at the size of the player’s deficit. A permit requires a losing personal record and a ladder interval wholly below 50%.
