# B1 · Ladder balance snapshot

- **Data approval.** SC2Pulse approved free publication of anonymous aggregate matchup statistics with attribution. The source confirms the first race gets the wins, null frames contain games of unknown duration, and cross-tier games count in both participating tiers. [Maintainer’s reply](https://github.com/sc2-pulse/sc2-pulse/discussions/758#discussioncomment-19597152)
- **Built.** The collector stores a complete season snapshot and coarse daily counts. The free `/balance` page shows same-tier win rates with counts and 95% Wilson intervals. It displays cross-tier results separately, with SC2Pulse credit, season and snapshot date. [ADR 0082](../../adr/0082-ladder-balance-snapshot.md)
- **Rollout.** On Render, set `SC2TOOLS_PULSE_BALANCE_ENABLED=1` and verify a complete response from `GET /v1/ladder/balance`. Season 68 is the initial mature season. Switch to season 69 only when all tracked regions reach the upstream collection window and validation passes.
