# B4 · Balance Court loss verdict

- **Built.** `/try#balance-court` reviews a ranked non-mirror 1v1 loss in the browser. It estimates a ladder cohort from regional rating bounds or the opponent’s league, with a manual override. The current ladder snapshot is the comparison, and the local exhibits show the loss autopsy and any comparable opponent evidence.
- **Sharing.** `/balance/verdict` resolves the exact pinned ladder snapshot from a fragment link. The link and PNG carry the verdict and aggregate ladder context. Replay details and exhibits stay local. Game duration does not enter the ruling.
