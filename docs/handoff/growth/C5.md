# C5 · Milestone share cards

- **Shipped.** The Notifications inbox lets players share an eligible ladder milestone as a PNG. Its share button is separate from the private game link.
- **Privacy.** The browser checks seven stored kind and headline combinations, including the existing 100 or 250 MMR steps. The card carries the headline, fixed ladder context and homepage link. The body, game link, identifiers and timestamps stay private.
- **Delivery.** The existing card controls handle delivery, retries and dismissals. Successful shares report only the fixed card kind, delivery method and normalized page state.
- **Tests.** 156 focused Vitest tests across 13 files, TypeScript, scoped ESLint and complexity, 25 UI rules, UI lint, help index and diff checks passed. Nine exact mutations failed their intended assertions and restored matching source hashes.
- **Browser.** Six offline Chromium cases at 360, 768 and 1280 px in light and dark verified keyboard focus, retry, dismissal, malformed headline rejection and a 1200 × 630 PNG. Sharing did not mark the notification as read or send an outbound request.
- **Build.** Next built `/notifications` at 185,266 of 520,000 gzip bytes, up 3,317 B from T3. The two inherited Windows overages fell 1 B each on overlay and dock.
- **Not verified.** A signed-in production account and the operating system share sheet. The fixture exercised real PNG generation with synthetic notifications.
- **References.** ADR 0051 records the sharing boundary. Local proof is in `C:/SC2TOOLS/.cache/growth-C5-tools` under `finish-02`, `mutation-02`, `build-01` and `browser-05`. C5 advances U1.
